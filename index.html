How to play

Move diagonally on dark squares. Regular pieces move and capture forward; kings move and capture both ways. With Mandatory captures ON, you must jump if a capture is available; OFF lets you make an ordinary move instead. In both modes, once a jump begins, continue every available jump with the same piece. If there is only one possible next jump, it continues automatically. If more than one route is possible, tap the glowing square you want. A regular checker that reaches the far king row ends its turn immediately, even if a backward jump would be possible as a king. Reach the far row to make a king. Win by capturing all opposing pieces or leaving the opponent with no legal move. Black moves first.
